# Mathematics Sources

Starting points for mathematical study, grouped by topic.
Verify current availability before recommending.

## Linear algebra

### MIT OpenCourseWare — Linear Algebra
- URL: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/
- Level: Undergraduate.
- Materials: Lectures, assignments, and exams.
- Useful for: A structured linear algebra course with practice.
- Access: Free; no registration.

## Mathematical problem solving

### Cambridge — NRICH
- URL: https://nrich.maths.org/
- Level: School mathematics, including advanced problems.
- Materials: Mathematical problems and supporting explanations.
- Useful for: Developing mathematical reasoning and problem-solving skills.
- Access: Free.

### Cambridge — STEP Support
- URL: https://step.maths.org/
- Level: Advanced school / university entrance.
- Materials: Preparation assignments and mathematical problems.
- Useful for: Transitioning to demanding university-style problems.
- Access: Free.

## Probability and statistics

### Berkeley — Open Textbooks
- URL: https://cdss.berkeley.edu/textbooks
- Materials: Online textbooks, including Prob 140, Data 8, and Data 100.
- Useful for: Finding probability and statistics resources;
  Data 8 and Data 100 also involve computing and data science.
- Access: Freely readable.
- Selection: Inspect the individual textbook for prerequisites
  and suitability; these books serve different levels.

## Optimization

### Stanford Engineering Everywhere
- URL: https://see.stanford.edu/Course
- Materials: Archived courses, including convex optimization.
- Useful for: Finding mathematically oriented optimization courses.
- Access: Free archived materials; some coursework may depend
  on unavailable university systems.
- Selection: Check the specific course's prerequisites and exercises.

## Logic and formal proof

### University of Pennsylvania — Software Foundations
- URL: https://softwarefoundations.cis.upenn.edu/
- Materials: Online textbooks and exercises.
- Useful for: Logic and machine-checked proofs in a
  computer-science setting.
- Access: Free; no enrollment.
- Scope: A specialized route into formal reasoning,
  not a general introduction to all mathematical proof.

## Applied and interdisciplinary mathematics

### Cornell — Networks, Crowds, and Markets
- URL: https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/kleinber/networks-book/
- Materials: Author-hosted textbook.
- Useful for: Network science, game theory, and their applications.
- Access: Freely downloadable.

### Imperial College London — Mathematics for Machine Learning
- URL: https://www.coursera.org/specializations/mathematics-machine-learning
- Materials: Online course specialization.
- Useful for: Mathematics studied in a machine-learning context.
- Access: Limited preview access; full free access is not established.
